A Little Letter ✉️

Tumhe pata hai, kabhi kabhi main sochta nahi, bas mehsoos karta hoon ki tum mere liye kya ho. Tumhari smile mere din ki shuruaat hai, aur tumhari aankhon mein dekhkar lagta hai jaise saare toofaan apne aap shaant ho gaye. Jis tarah tum mera naam leti ho, usme ek sukoon hai jo main shabdon mein kabhi nahi bata paunga. 🥺 Tumne mujhe tab bhi sambhala jab main khud ko nahi sambhal paaya. Tumhari khamoshi bhi mujhe samajh jaati hai, aur tumhara "main hoon na" mere liye duniya ki sabse badi taskeen hai. Tumhare random texts, late night baatein aur voice notes mere din ke sabse pyaare hisse hain. Tum meri choti se choti baat yaad rakhti/rakhte ho, aur poochte ho "khana khaya?", tab lagta hai main kisi ke dil mein sach mein basa hoon. 🤍 Tumne mere andhere ko bhi apnaya, meri kamiyon ke saath bhi mujhe chuna, aur aaj bhi roz chun rahi/rahe ho. Tumhari ziddi aadatein, tumhari choti si jealousy, tumhari sharm aur tumhari himmat, mujhe tumhari har cheez se pyaar hai, tumhari kamiyon se bhi. Tumhare saath khamoshi mein baithna bhi mujhe ghar jaisa lagta hai. 🏡 Main har dua mein tumhe yaad rakhta hoon, aur chahta hoon ki tum humesha muskurate raho. Kyunki tum jaise ho, waise hi kaafi ho, aur mere liye sab kuch ho. 💖 💛

Always yours,
Aryan
